Decision efficiency screening — October 30, 2026

All four new ten-turn trials completed with complete all-role accounting, paired
with four reused controls. Total tokens fell 30.80% for Luna and 9.13% for Terra;
uncached input fell 19.19% and 15.63%. Growth and confirmed military losses matched
in every pair. Other outcomes varied, with an additional Terra conquest and an
earlier Luna peace. These results support retaining the delivery improvements,
but do not prove equal gameplay quality or isolate the effect of each change.

Purpose

Reduce repeated input and avoidable inspections while retaining useful preloads,
native action checks and independent Main, Economy and Military agents. This
change follows the worker-ownership, Military-completion and plan-intent changes
already committed at ed72e4275a7cee570478a4bd63e1344351eeca8d.

Implemented changes

1. Keep unchanged inspected details across sibling actions, while fencing stale
   decisions until a fresh observation arrives. Compare the exact requested
   projection; invalidate changed details, all affected ownership labels and
   revision-dependent previews. Economy and Military also retain the delivered
   overview across their own successful actions, allowing exact state deltas.
2. Military may request its completion review with the last chosen action using
   reviewAfter=true, or combine military_review with independent inspections.
   Completion-recovery replies already contain the first page. Native actions,
   authored holds and freshness checks are unchanged. Failed refreshes preserve
   confirmed receipts and never cause automatic reexecution.
3. Consolidate repeated protocol instructions and use compact role resumes only
   when the model has a known delivered baseline. Host-only reads, restart,
   compaction recovery and lost baselines still require full delivery.
4. Preserve existing decision preloads. Add citizens/production detail for up to
   two selected Economy cities with an empty construction queue, starvation or
   unassigned citizens. Routine city passes and worker-only passes do not gain
   those details. Wonders retain the available names and optional detail reads.

Worker ownership, concurrency, native advice, game rules and model effort were
not changed by this stage. Existing benchmark runtimes were not replaced.

No-call replay evidence

The same four captured contexts were replayed for each of the three roles:
12 contexts and 137 offered commands per implementation. Both implementations
had zero schema errors, ownership errors, mandatory-information changes or
subsequent-read errors. Median startup characters fell 0.63% for Main, 0.67% for
Economy and 0.18% for Military. The existing preload was deliberately retained;
the expected savings are mainly in repeated responses and requests.

An additional replay used the exact successful specialist action outcomes from
the reused live controls. Retaining each delivered baseline reduced 159 serialized
receipts from 5,770,820 to 333,025 characters (94.23%). Every resulting overview
reconstructed exactly, including the original invalidation lists; no updates
failed reconstruction and no eligible full action lacked a baseline. This is
receipt serialization evidence, not a 94% total-token saving. It makes no model
calls and does not assume that future models choose the same actions.

  updates. All 193 confirmed candidate native commands across Main, Economy and

These are durable tool counts, not model-request counts. Bundled children are

Communication follow-up: focused observations and conditional preloads

This is a separate offline screen. The live trials above had native-AI opponents
and did not exercise communication jobs. No paid model calls were made for this
follow-up, and the previous live results are unchanged.

Implementation

New communication activations default to player_turn(view="communication").
Startup still begins with chat_read; an empire overview is not added to every
message. When requested, the focused overview retains native relationships,
treaties, economic constraints, pending decisions, threats, visibility limits,
research, victory progress and summaries of every owned city and unit. It omits
unrelated construction, settlement and wonder menus. Omitted information is named
with retrieval instructions; full permitted observations remain available. View
changes reset delivery baselines, and refreshes, recovery and receipt replay use
the same projection. Communication gains no gameplay command authority.

Up to two pending shared offers are inspected through ordinary trade_read calls
when new events identify the proposal or its counterparty. Historical thread
excerpts, own-message echoes, native-AI offers and a public message's whole
audience do not trigger unrelated preloads. Successful prepared details provide
the same exact version and evidence as an explicit read. Failed or subsequently
changed offers still require inspection; unchanged freshness and decision checks
reject unobserved or stale acceptance. All other pending offers keep their exact
inspection commands.

Active accepted war goals now enter communication's existing bounded working
context. Unaccepted revisions appear as counts and exact retrieval references;
they do not replace the accepted goals. Oversized or omitted episodes remain
retrievable through the read-only war_goals tool. Exact authored history and
nonbinding social promises are preserved. Relevant tool definitions are prepared
only when needed. Chat deltas and repeated-chat suppression remain follow-up work.
