Gameplay ownership, military completion, and plan citations — 2026-10-29

All twelve planned ten-turn trials completed with full all-role accounting:
120 own turns. One additional worker-only attempt halted after eight turns and
was rerun from the original board; the unresolved halt is documented below.

Worker ownership and plan-only intent show useful improvements in this screen.
The Military completion review makes remaining risks explicit, but these results
do not establish better tactics. The combined candidate used 56.49% more total
tokens for Luna and 15.83% fewer for Terra across the two positions. Runtime is
excluded from these conclusions. The current gameplay tree matches the frozen
candidate, all evaluator authorizations were removed, and existing benchmark
runtimes remain pinned.

Scope and implementation

The previous working tree, including the role-context implementation and its
completed evaluation reports, was committed first as dcd47a2. The selected
follow-ups are implemented in 751a428, abe70b0 and 9496966, with worker scheduling
and representation corrections in eb1ffc1 and b693070. Reporting support is in
172d7f1 and cae4f60. No engine changes were made.

1. Economy owns ordinary unescorted workers. Native worker capability determines
   membership; command advertising and execution share the ownership function.
   An explicit escort assignment gives Main both units, and release restores
   the ordinary Economy/Military owners. Linked movement, swaps, and native
   escort relationships retain their ownership checks. New/idle workers, ended
   jobs or journeys, and changed visible danger can wake Economy. Worker-only
   passes can select zero cities without resetting the full city-review clock.

2. Military receives a compact completion review of controlled units near
   visible opponents, native attack opportunities, reachable visible civilian
   captures, and city bombardment options. A fresh factual basis and reasoned
   holds cover unresolved targets at finish_role. Deliberate risk remains valid;
   the host does not require attacking, retreating, or meeting a strategic goal.
   Observed changes and operation retrieval help the model reassess progress
   without treating missing units as combat losses or counts as success.

5. A city command can supply intent.cityPlan without repeating intent.reason.
   The audit pins the exact delivered matching-city plan version, leaves absent
   prose blank, and confirms the link only after a successful native receipt.
   Unverified references receive feedback without inventing a rationale or
   changing native legality. Whitespace-only annotations remain invalid.

Main, Economy, and Military remain separate concurrent roles. Native-advice
settings, gameplay rules, and existing benchmark runtimes remain unchanged.
Sequential execution and the unselected gameplay recommendations are not part
of this change.

Screening design

This follows the earlier 32-trial context-isolation evaluation recorded in
Role-context-live-followup-2026-10-29.txt. It compares three frozen Python source
variants:

  baseline  dcd47a2, the committed role-context implementation
  workers   751a428 plus the saved worker-scope and worker-field fixes
  candidate b693070, all three selected follow-ups plus both fixes

The worker variant isolates ownership from the other follow-ups. The candidate
adds Military completion and plan-only intent together; their individual effects
are not independently estimated by this screen.

Two prepared Pangaea Inca positions are shared unchanged across variants: g1
starts at native turn 135 (audit source turn 134), and g4 starts at 156. Each variant receives ten own
turns under gpt-6-luna and gpt-6.1-terra, with one repetition per position/model:
12 trials and 120 scheduled own turns. Each starts with empty private memory;
other civilizations are controlled by native AI. Models use medium reasoning,
both guides enabled, review interval 10, Economy/Military intervals 5, and no
native advice or adaptive effort. The engine is pinned to
63e4bee9e6cefff0be88abd2c5ec4f6e0b6793dd.

One repetition on two positions is screening evidence. Model decisions and
native outcomes can differ; these results do not establish causation, equal
quality, or win rate. Runtime is recorded only for operations and has no weight
in the gameplay or efficiency conclusions, as requested.

Measurement

All-role usage includes Main, Economy, Military, reviews, and consultations.
Input includes cached input once; total tokens are input plus output. Reasoning
output is a subset of output and is not added again. The three pairwise
comparisons share trials and must not be summed as independent cohorts.

Population, buildings, technologies, units, and improvements use the original
native save differences. Retained-city growth excludes acquired populations.
New improvements count previously unimproved retained tiles, excluding territory
acquisition; replacements, roads, repairs, and unfinished jobs need the detailed
evidence. Worker orders count confirmed commands, not completed improvements.
Losses use native combat reports, not disappearance from a roster. Building and
technology counts do not weight their value, and buildings may be purchased.
Science and production are dated rates, not cumulative yields.

Endpoint reads use isolated copies and do not advance the turn. The evaluator
accepts their rates only if native state matches after normalizing researched
technology order and explored-tile reload bookkeeping. A comparison uses native
endpoints only when both reads pass; otherwise both use dated last-agent reads.

Results and interpretation

Worker-only: new improvements rose from 4 to 7 across Luna's two positions and
from 9 to 10 across Terra's. Total tokens changed by +5.54% and -12.95%,
respectively. Luna issued 12 confirmed worker orders instead of 2; Terra issued
15 in both variants. This illustrates why more orders and more improvements are
separate measures. In Terra's war position, the completed worker-only run took
Paris and Orleans, matching baseline's territorial gains, with one reported
military loss instead of two. The interrupted worker-only attempt remains a
reliability caveat to this favorable completed run.

Plan-only intent: the combined candidates confirmed ten commands with a plan
citation and no duplicated reason, with zero argument-schema rejections of that
shape. The baseline rejected nine such calls and the worker-only controls seven.
Other freshness/legality errors remain independent checks. Sampled confirmed
receipts retained the exact delivered plan version and an empty reason field.

Military completion: the candidates made 48 completion reads, recovered from
five stale/incomplete completion reviews, and recorded 29 grouped hold reasons.
The holds include declining expensive melee attacks and preserving wounded
units. These are evidence that the protocol works, not that the decisions are
optimal. Relative to worker-only, combined usage changed by +48.28% for Luna and
-3.30% for Terra; the candidate also includes the intent fix, and decisions differ,
so these are not isolated estimates of checklist overhead.

Luna's war baseline and worker-only runs accepted 275 gold for peace on turn 157.
The combined candidate's peace consultation declined that offer, continued
fighting, and accepted 88 gold on turn 161. Each lost one military unit and took
no cities. The candidate recorded 13 confirmed combat results, versus one for
baseline and zero for worker-only, but additional fighting is not itself better
progress. Its longer war also contributed more gameplay work to the comparison.
The completion review caught exposure after actions: one crossbowman fired at
7 HP and then had no movement left to retreat. Earlier planning of the ending
position is a plausible refinement; the completion check cannot undo spent moves.

Terra's combined candidate took Paris with one reported military loss; baseline
took Paris and Orleans with two losses, and worker-only took both with one loss.
Thus the combined changes do not establish a better military outcome here.
Baseline's total population gain of 21 in that position includes conquest;
starting-city growth was 5, versus the candidate's 6 (14 total with conquest).
In the early Luna position, baseline explicitly enabled avoid-growth in Machu
and completed an additional university. Growth differences are not automatically
worker effects. This screen supports testing worker ownership further and
retaining the schema repair; it does not justify a claim of overall superiority.

Interrupted attempts and diagnostics

The first live attempt exposed a compact-observation field collision: a worker
capability boolean used the native worker-detail object's field name. Strategic
review publication then tried to read object fields from that boolean. The
corrected implementation uses canBuildImprovements for compact capability and
preserves the native worker object. Regression coverage includes publishing a
review with the worker roster. The failed attempt and its accounting remain in
the build artifacts; every corrected trial restarted from the same prepared
board and empty memory. No failed or interrupted trial is treated as a completed
comparison. A separate reporting fix tolerates rejected string-valued intent
arguments when counting durable calls; it does not change gameplay.

Seven attempts started before that restart. Their recorded all-role totals are
34,533,936 input tokens (29,508,224 cached; 5,025,712 uncached), 111,829 output
tokens, and 800 model requests. One interrupted Luna worker attempt has incomplete
accounting, so these are recorded totals rather than a complete cost estimate.
They are kept separate from corrected-trial outcome and usage comparisons.

The corrected suite also had one native-host halt: Terra's g4 worker-only trial
stopped after eight completed rounds, during the attempted advancement of turn
164. GAME_HALTED on the coordinator's END_TURN preceded the communication failures
and role interruptions. The original in-memory mutation exception was not
persisted, so its root cause remains unknown. A trusted diagnostic loaded a copy
of the saved checkpoint and accounting ledger, then advanced 164 to 165 without
error. That reload does not rule out a defect in the original in-memory state.

Eleven completed trials were retained. The missing worker-only trial was rerun
from the original prepared board with empty memory and identical frozen
source/settings, under live-retry, and completed all ten turns with full accounting.
Its predecessor and all-role accounting remain
in priorInterruptedAttempts in the detailed report. This is a reliability caveat,
not evidence that the failure was harmless or caused by provider compute.
That halted attempt recorded 21,138,681 input tokens (19,524,224 cached;
1,614,457 uncached), 39,242 output tokens, and 377 requests. Its incomplete
accounting remains marked incomplete and is excluded from complete-pair totals.
