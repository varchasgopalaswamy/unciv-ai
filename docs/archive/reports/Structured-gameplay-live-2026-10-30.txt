Structured gameplay live test — October 30, 2026

Follow-up usage audit and receipt-compaction fix are recorded at the end.

The candidate executed real gameplay on Luna and Terra, but neither trial met
the requested three-round test horizon. Luna completed two rounds; Terra
completed one and part of its second. Both reached the reported-token stop
threshold. Terra also recorded a failed state refresh after a confirmed attack

evidence that structured decisions execute and confirmed actions survive a
refresh failure. It is not evidence of improved gameplay quality or efficiency.

Measured contributors and limits

1. Luna generated 28 responses containing disabled exec attempts, among its 74
   reported responses. Those responses consumed 1,329,522 input tokens,
   including 1,107,072 cached, and 3,389 output tokens. These are real failed-tool
   responses, not host receipt replays or duplicate accounting. Preventing the
   attempts would change later decisions, so these numbers are not a measured
   counterfactual saving. Four jobs attempted exec on their first response,
   before receiving decision_receipts; receipt continuation alone therefore
   cannot explain the behavior. No attempted exec ran. Terra had none.

2. Terra's Military cost grew even though its aggregate first-turn total did
   not. Military input increased from 764,162 to 1,421,861; responses rose from
   15 to 17, while mean input per response rose from 50,944 to 83,639 tokens.
   Its first new context was 37,994 tokens and its last was 112,278.
   All 14 model-requested unit_details reads in that turn explicitly selected
   delivery=full. It also requested two full player_turn observations and a
   full radius-four map. Full delivery intentionally bypasses delta/sharing
   savings. The previous run used full delivery for nine of 18 model-requested
   unit detail reads, no full player_turn, and an ordinary radius-three map.
   The new Main and Economy costs fell, masking this Military regression in
   the total. This does not establish equivalent gameplay quality.

3. The complete schemas add a fixed context cost despite removing duplicated
   startup schema listings. First-response input grew by approximately 3.4K
   tokens for Economy, 4.8K for Military and 8.8K for Main on each model. These
   are actual net request-size changes, not isolated schema-token measurements.
   Current schemas are 50,623 / 60,856 / 92,695 serialized characters for
   Economy / Military / Main. Character sizes must not be described as tokens.
   Generic Codex coding and multi-agent instructions were also present in both
   old and new traces; they are overhead, not a newly introduced cost.

4. The new runs had 21 partial batches and 30 unexecuted children, which require
   reconsideration or fresh observation. These include real concurrent state
   changes and ownership/prerequisite safeguards. Removing those checks merely
   to reduce calls would violate the protocol. The audit does not attribute an
   exact token saving to eliminating any particular partial batch.

Confirmed local fix

DecisionLoop.execute expanded the gateway's compact response before retaining
and delivering its child receipt. It now preserves the exact gateway response;
delivered() still expands the child for host inspection bookkeeping. This also
preserves explicit full-delivery requests and durable replay behavior. It does
not change schemas, legal actions, ownership, or any gameplay guidance.

The saved gateway responses expand exactly to all 179 delivered child receipts.
Preserving them would remove 4,997 serialized characters from Luna's receipt
payloads (587,508 to 582,511), before accounting for repeated context. Terra's
826,686 receipt characters are unchanged because its delivered replies were
already expanded. This is a small fix, not a solution to the measured regression.
Recompacting explicit full replies could reduce them further but would disregard
the requested delivery semantics; the fix does not do that.

The regression test failed at the compact-receipt equality assertion before the
fix. Afterward, 64 decision, recovery, failure-tracking and payload tests passed,
including compact/full delivery, exact reconstruction, delivered inspection
credit and restart replay. Repository Ruff lint and formatting checks passed;
Pyrefly reported zero errors and 40 warnings; git diff --check passed. The full
Python suite was not rerun for this change. The frozen live candidate and raw
evidence remain unchanged. No additional live model calls were made.

The candidate has not demonstrated an efficiency improvement and remains
isolated. The next substantive work is to prevent unavailable-tool continuation
loops and unnecessary full observations, then measure a bounded comparison.
No saving from those future changes is claimed here.
