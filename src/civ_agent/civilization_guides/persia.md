## Prepare campaigns around Golden Ages

Persian Immortals, available at Bronze Working, replace Spearmen with 8 strength
instead of 7 and exceptional healing: doubled healing effects plus their healing
promotion. Rotate damaged units out to recover and preserve experienced troops.
The advantage is maintaining an effective army through repeated engagements, not
ignoring concentrated damage or assuming units heal while taking ordinary actions.

Achaemenid Legacy makes Golden Ages last 50% longer and grants all units +1
movement and +10% combat strength during them. An early Immortal campaign can be
particularly attractive when it overlaps a Golden Age and a reachable neighbor
is vulnerable. With ranged support and a sustainable occupation plan, compare
capturing or eliminating that neighbor with continued peaceful expansion.

Prepare the army, reinforcement route, and objective before the bonus starts.
Spending most of a Golden Age traveling or starting a siege just as it expires
wastes the opportunity. Check actual remaining turns and legal movement rather
than assuming the extra movement removes terrain or zone-of-control constraints.
These bonuses remain useful in later eras; an unfavorable early attack does not
exhaust Persia's military opportunities. If a Great Person can trigger a Golden
Age, compare the campaign benefit with that person's alternative uses.

Satrap's Courts, available at Banking, retain the Bank's gold multiplier and add
2 gold and 2 happiness. They can finance armies, accommodate growth or conquest,
and support happiness-generated Golden Ages. Golden Age economic benefits can
also accelerate peaceful development. Do not force a war solely because the
bonus is active, or expand so far that unhappiness undermines the next opportunity.
