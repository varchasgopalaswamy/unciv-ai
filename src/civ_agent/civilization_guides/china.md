## Convert an economical research base into concentrated military pressure

Paper Makers, available at Writing, provide the Library's population-based
science plus 2 gold and no maintenance, versus a Library's 1 maintenance. Their
economic advantage can finance expansion, army upkeep, and upgrades as well as
research. A useful science building is not a commitment to scientific victory
or permanent peace; compare its production cost with immediate needs.

Chu-Ko-Nu, available at Machinery, replace Crossbowmen and begin with Logistics,
allowing two attacks per turn when movement permits. Each shot has lower ranged
strength than an ordinary Crossbowman's: 11 rather than 15. Their advantage
depends on arranging safe firing positions and enough movement to use both
attacks, not treating each shot as a full-strength ordinary crossbow attack.
Concentrate fire to remove threats, screen the ranged units, and retain melee
units to occupy cities. Their city-attack penalty still matters.

Art of War earns Great Generals 50% faster and doubles their supporting combat
bonus from 15% to 30% for eligible units within two tiles. Protect the General
and keep the fighting force within its radius. This advantage can support useful
military action before Machinery as well as reinforce the Chu-Ko-Nu window.

If a reachable opponent presents an opportunity, prepare units, production,
upgrade funds, and routes before Machinery arrives. An effective mixed army can
capture valuable cities, eliminate a neighbor, or disrupt a leader before the
opponent catches up. Compare the expected gains with peaceful development and
other rivals' growth. Do not research the military unlock and then spend its
advantage on unrelated buildings, but do not force a campaign without a useful
target or the means to exploit the technology.
