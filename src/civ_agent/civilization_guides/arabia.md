## Convert trade wealth into a mobile army

Bazaars, available at Currency, retain the Market's gold benefits, provide an
extra copy of each improved local luxury resource, and add 2 gold to worked Oil
and Oasis tiles. Improve useful luxuries and compare trading surplus copies for
income or missing luxuries with keeping them for later negotiations. Duplicate
luxuries do not themselves provide additional happiness, and trade revenue
depends on finding willing buyers; do not assume a guaranteed market.

Trade Caravans adds 1 gold to each city connection to the capital in this Vanilla
ruleset. It is not the later-expansion caravan-unit system. Compare connection
income and reinforcement value with road upkeep, worker time, and vulnerability.
Desert start bias does not make every bare desert city site productive.

Camel Archers, available at Chivalry, replace Knights with ranged units that can
move after attacking. Secure horses and prepare production or upgrade funds
before the opportunity arrives. Use favorable approaches and available movement
to fire and retreat, concentrate damage, and wear down enemy armies. They receive
no defensive terrain bonus and are vulnerable when caught; check legal retreat
routes and do not assume every shot leaves enough movement to escape. Bring
melee units to capture cities, since Camel Archers cannot occupy them themselves.

A Chivalry-era campaign can turn economic strength into captured land or remove
a competitor. Compare it with continued trade and development, including any
imports or customers lost through war. Maintaining some profitable relationships
does not require peace with every rival. Arabia also doubles Oil resource
production, supporting a larger later oil-dependent force if production and
maintenance permit. Extra oil alone is not a reason to build units without a
concrete purpose or neglect nearer opportunities.
