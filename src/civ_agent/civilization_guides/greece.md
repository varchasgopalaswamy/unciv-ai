## Exploit early military strength and durable city-state relationships

Hoplites, available at Bronze Working, have 9 strength versus the ordinary
Spearman's 7. Companion Cavalry, available at Horseback Riding, have 12 strength
and 5 movement versus the Horseman's 10 and 4, and earn Great Generals faster.
These are complementary early advantages, not a reason to postpone all military
decisions until a late-game victory race.

Scout nearby opponents and approaches early. With horses, a reachable target,
supporting units, and room in the happiness budget, eliminating or severely
weakening a neighbor before these advantages disappear can be worth more than
another round of peaceful infrastructure. Captured land can support any eventual
victory. Compare that opportunity with settlement and city-state investment;
a distant or well-defended target can make an early campaign a poor bargain.

Companion Cavalry still suffer a city-attack penalty and receive no defensive
terrain bonus. Use mobility to defeat field units, intercept reinforcements,
and withdraw from exposure. Bring ranged or siege support when needed and retain
a force that can occupy the target city. Horses and safe reinforcement routes
matter as much as unlocking the unit.

Hellenic League halves city-state influence degradation, doubles recovery toward
the resting point, and treats city-state territory as friendly. Quests and
selective investment in useful relationships can therefore provide lasting
resources and other benefits. They can sustain conquest as well as a diplomatic
finish. Influence is still contested; do not scatter gifts without assessing
their payoff or conquer a useful city-state simply because it is nearby.
