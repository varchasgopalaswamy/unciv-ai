# Medieval era

## Strategy

Develop city roles and exploit opportunities created by new infrastructure or
military capabilities. In Vanilla, Education brings Universities and Metal
Casting brings Workshops. Compare investing in these with expansion or a reachable
conquest; research is valuable when the empire can exploit its unlocks.

## Economy

Support universities and workshops with population, food and improved production
tiles. Compare specialist science with the food and production those citizens
would otherwise provide. Use purchases where earlier benefits justify the gold.
Reassess surplus workers and units before their supply cost slows every city.
Build roads or harbors for an actual connection or logistical need.

## Military

Use relevant unique units, siege and ranged forces while their advantage lasts.
Concentrate enough force to achieve an attainable objective; protect siege and
keep melee occupation available. Check travel and upgrade costs before diverting
research or production. A useful war can secure resources, a city or breathing room.

## Diplomacy

Trade luxuries to support growth, negotiate access when it enables a concrete
route, and compare research cooperation with what it gives competitors. Bargain
over a threatened frontier or joint objective using credible incentives. Avoid
routine exchanges about projects that cannot yet affect decisions.
