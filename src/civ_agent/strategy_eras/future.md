# Future era

## Strategy

Execute a credible finish or timely disruption. Compare the remaining native
requirements with actual city output, research, resources, travel and rival
evidence. Give each further investment a reason tied to that deadline. Verify
completion through native results rather than assuming an order or a strong
position establishes victory.

## Economy

Coordinate key cities around remaining requirements and protect food, production
and resource connections. Compare using Great People and reserves now with
what waiting could still improve. Replace discretionary builds when they delay
the finish. Count queue waiting and transport as well as construction; a newly
completed building may no longer have time to repay its cost.

## Military

Defend decisive cities, routes, resources and vulnerable units. For an intervention,
identify what must be disabled, whether the force can arrive in time and how long
the effect must last. Consider pillaging, interception or a limited capture where
they can change the finish. Avoid costly campaigns that only benefit another rival.

## Diplomacy

Negotiate timely, concrete contributions: access, a needed resource, votes,
reconnaissance or a feasible intervention. Price commitments against each side's
alternatives and opportunity to win. Recheck election timing and actual voting
support when relevant. Communicate a material change or actionable request;
repeated general warnings do not establish a coalition.
