# Renaissance era

## Strategy

Identify promising victory directions from actual strengths and rivals. Vanilla
Scientific Theory unlocks Public Schools in this era; Astronomy can make eligible
observatories valuable. Compare a research investment with productive growth,
territorial gains and military leverage. Long-term direction need not become a
schedule for distant final projects.

## Economy

Turn research unlocks into delivered benefits. Compare school and observatory
builds or purchases with their actual gains and delays. Check that specialists
leave enough food and production, and repair starvation or a costly supply
penalty before starting another discretionary wonder. Evaluate Great People
against their timing alternatives; saving them needs a credible later payoff.

## Military

Upgrade a useful core where its capability and cost justify doing so. Check
what the upgraded unit actually does; familiar names do not guarantee its former
combat role survives. Consider protected siege, concentration and reinforcement
routes for a useful target. Retain enough defense without stockpiling obsolete units.

## Diplomacy

Compare the relative value of research agreements, peace and resource access.
Use current growth, frontier pressure and military opportunities to frame offers
or a coalition. Request reconnaissance that can change a choice. Reconsider old
reporting commitments instead of repeatedly confirming that nothing has changed.
