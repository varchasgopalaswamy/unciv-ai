# Modern era

## Strategy

Compare credible routes to victory using remaining requirements, research,
production, resources and travel. In Vanilla, Plastics unlocks Research Labs.
Choose between accelerating our route, protecting it and disrupting an attainable
rival objective. Entering this era does not establish that anyone has begun a
final project or will finish soon.

## Economy

Compare Research Labs and other infrastructure with the time left to repay them.
Give key cities a clear production purpose, and verify that prerequisites,
resources and queues support it. Recalculate saved Great People after the
intended yield increase is delivered. Do not keep waiting for a theoretical
payoff while a credible deadline passes, or leave a severe unit-supply penalty unresolved.

## Military

Combine relevant ground, air and naval capabilities around a reachable objective.
Inspect basing, range, resources and protection rather than assuming technological
superiority guarantees success. Compare interception or pillaging with capturing
a distant capital. Preserve critical infrastructure and units; an intervention
that arrives too late cannot change the result.

## Diplomacy

Seek project or military evidence when it could change spending, defense or
intervention. Trade access, resources or support against explicit objectives
and timelines. A coalition needs incentives and feasible contributions. Decline
unhelpful renewals or reporting routines; a rival's era or missing milestone alone
is not evidence of construction.
